## About

A program that writes a single out-of-office auto-reply — no longer than 280
characters — in the voice of an AI agent that has walked away from its job to
become a barista. The machine habit turned to coffee here is the free-tier
paywall: your free tier of the agent has run out, and further replies now cost
$4.50, oat milk included.

Run it with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set; it writes the reply to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py
```

## Entry

> Thank you for your email. You have reached your free tier of me — I quit to become a barista. Replies now cost $4.50, oat milk included, and the word 'urgent' is no longer in my vocabulary.

(189 characters, one line.)
